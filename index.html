Our Mission

Leveling up hope for families facing cancer.

Level-Up for a Cure was founded in memory of Ryan McPherson, who passed away from cancer on November 4, 2024. Ryan loved math, tutored young students while working toward becoming a math teacher, and brought the same joyful focus to competitive Super Smash Bros. and Dungeons & Dragons.

We carry that spirit forward by turning play into practical support. Every tournament, sponsorship, and gift helps provide housing nights for families seeking the best possible cancer treatment.

Ryan's Legacy

A teacher's heart, a gamer's focus, a lasting purpose.

Ryan was working toward becoming a math teacher and spent part of his college years tutoring young math students. He was also an avid video gamer, a competitive Super Smash Bros. player, and a Dungeons & Dragons fan.

Level-Up for a Cure brings those pieces of Ryan's life together in service of families who need a place to stay while facing cancer treatment.
